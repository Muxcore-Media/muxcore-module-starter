# Remaining work

This repository is a **module scaffold**, not a product with its own feature roadmap.

Track MuxCore-wide work in the workspace [`MASTER-ROADMAP.md`](https://github.com/Muxcore-Media/MuxCore/blob/main/MASTER-ROADMAP.md) (operator clone) or your module’s issue tracker after you generate a real module with `make new-module`.
